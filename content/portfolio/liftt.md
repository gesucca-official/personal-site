All hail linear algebra. Seriously, I hadn’t touched it since university and was perfectly happy leaving matrices, vectors, and vector norms in the past. But here I was, neck-deep in all that gibberish again.

And Matlab? Don’t get me started. I despise that language with a passion, but for this project, it was a necessary evil to handle the complexity of the calculations.

From diving into an article about the importance of metacentric height in floating bodies to breaking my head over a 3D equilibrium problem, this project has been one wild ride.

Thank goodness for my excellent mechanical high school education—without it, I’m not sure I could’ve pulled this off. A more generalist education might not have cut it!
